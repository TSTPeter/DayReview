What this spelling game keeps, and where

For grown-ups. Written 1 October 2026. A draft for review.

The short version

Everything your child does in the game stays on your tablet. Nobody else sees it, including the people who made the game. There are no accounts, no adverts, no tracking and no analytics.

On your tablet

The game keeps your child's answers, their practice schedule and their game scores in the browser on this tablet. If you type a first name in the grown-up view, that stays on the tablet too. In the grown-up view you can export all of it, or delete all of it.

Only if you switch them on

Sync. Off unless you set it up with your own Firebase project. Then only counts and percentages are sent, never a word your child wrote. Your child's welcome page tells them when it is on.

Microphone. Off unless you switch it on. In the pattern game it listens for your child saying a word aloud. It only measures how loud the room is, on the tablet. Nothing is recorded, kept or sent, and it is never turned into words.

Sharing

The button on the welcome page, "Share this with your friends", sends the game's address and one plain sentence: through your device's own share sheet, by copying it, or as a QR code. The grown-up view has a similar message for other parents. Nothing about your child goes with it, there is no tracking, and nobody is told who shared it. A friend who opens it starts fresh, and the two children cannot see each other. Which apps and contacts your device's share sheet offers is the device's business, not this game's.
